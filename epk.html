Biography

Bio

VO!D is here to dropkick you in the chest. A 3 piece built on groove, brutality, and depth. We began to release music under this name in 2025, but our members have been writing together for 10 years.

RESIDUAL came together from a place of stepping forward, and leaving something behind. It was written to make a statement. “When we die, that’s it.” To remain in place is to decay, and to move forward is just the same. We’d rather move forward.

RESIDUAL was written, recorded, edited, and produced by VO!D, and mixed and mastered by our friend Tyler Corbett at Sole Audio. Living statically on the internet as an artifact of who we were that year.

We are currently in prep to begin executing our second EP. Writing is nearly complete, and recording is underway. A majority of these songs are used in our current live set. We are excited to bring the next chapter of our sound to the world, and hope you’re ready.
