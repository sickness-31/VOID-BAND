The Band

About

Calgary's VO!D takes influence from all things heavy & groovy, seeking to demonstrate uniquely raw & crushing riffs, as well as groovy, forward-pushing drums, and loud, harsh vocals, blended into a wall of sound that moves you.

Certainly small and upcoming, we love to get a room moving.
