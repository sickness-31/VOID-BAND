About

Calgary Alberta’s VO!D takes influence from all things heavy & groovy. Uniquely raw, crushing riffs, as well as tasty, forward drums, & loud, harsh vocals, all blended into a wall of sound that moves you.

Certainly small and upcoming, we love to get a room moving.
